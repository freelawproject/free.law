## Why we need you

Free Law Project works to provide free, public, and permanent access to court records and case law, and we build tools that make that access useful. Our upcoming project will bring a new level of access to the courts, providing a vital resource to researchers, scholars, and the public. Before we release it, we need volunteers to review a portion of the data and resolve errors.

The task sounds simple, but it matters. People rely on our resources to understand what the courts do and don’t say, so every error you catch makes our materials more trustworthy for everyone who uses them.

## What you’ll need

- A computer and a reliable internet connection
- A good eye for detail
- The ability to read English and understand basic grammar
- As much time as you’d like to give, from a few hours to 50 or more

*No experience is needed.* We’ll provide all the training you need during onboarding.
