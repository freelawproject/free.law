<p className="lead">Free Law Project announced today that its CourtListener Model Context Protocol (MCP) server is now available within <a href="https://cloud.google.com/ai/legal">Google Cloud's Gemini Enterprise for Legal solution</a>.</p>

Free Law Project's MCP server gives AI agents on Gemini Enterprise grounded, verifiable access to CourtListener's full legal research platform. That includes case law records, federal court filings, citation networks, oral argument transcripts, case and query alerts, and more.

Giving Gemini Enterprise access to these resources unlocks advanced legal research:

- A litigation team can ask an agent to surface and summarize every circuit split on a doctrine.
- A compliance-minded firm can verify every citation in an incoming brief against primary sources before it goes out the door.
- A team tracking dozens of cases can set alerts for when any of them cite specific case law.

In short, tasks that legal professionals currently do by hand can now be delegated to Gemini Enterprise to do the work. How to get started and full documentation on what the MCP can do is available on [Free Law Project's wiki][wiki].

Reaching enterprise legal teams through Gemini Enterprise for Legal means more organizations, including large firms and corporate legal departments, can easily get direct access to primary-source legal data instead of an AI model's best guess. That is exactly the kind of reach Free Law Project's open infrastructure was built to make possible.

"Our goal at Free Law Project is to bring high-quality legal research to everybody. Today's announcement brings grounded, verifiable legal information to enterprise legal teams right inside the tools they already use every day," said Michael Lissner, Executive Director of Free Law Project.

"Integrating CourtListener into Gemini Enterprise for Legal bridges the gap between advanced AI and verifiable legal truth. Together with Free Law Project, we are delivering the speed, trust, and transparency that legal departments and law firms demand," said Satish Thomas, Vice President, Google Cloud.

Legal teams interested in connecting CourtListener to their own Gemini Enterprise for Legal environments can find setup details in [Free Law Project's documentation][wiki], or reach out with [Group Membership questions][membership] directly.
